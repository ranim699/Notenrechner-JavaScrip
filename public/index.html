      How the grade is calculated

      • Grades: 1,0 · 1,3 · 1,7 · 2,0 · 2,3 · 2,7 · 3,0 · 3,3 · 3,7 · 4,0 · 5,0. Passed with 4,0 or better.
      • The final grade is the average of all graded modules, weighted by credit points.
      • Only the first decimal counts – the rest is cut off, not rounded (2,38 → 2,3).
      • Ungraded modules (Lern- u. Arbeitstechniken, Technisches Englisch, Seminar Methodik, Praxissemester) do not count.
      • Thesis 80 % + colloquium 20 %, together 15 CP.
      • Up to 1,5 very good · up to 2,5 good · up to 3,5 satisfactory · up to 4,0 sufficient.

      Sources: Rahmenprüfungsordnung FH Dortmund § 9 and § 35; Studiengangsprüfungsordnung Wirtschaftsinformatik § 33, § 35, Anlage 1 and 2. Your grades are only saved in this browser.
